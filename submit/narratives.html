1. THE PROBLEM

Canadian media shapes how millions think about aging. Our analysis of 11,929 articles from 2023-2026 reveals a critical narrative crisis:

18.4% of coverage uses harmful "limiting" language that frames seniors as burdens or crises.

This systematic bias:

  • 🔴 Influences policy decisions about senior services
  • 🔴 Shapes public perception of older adults' value
  • 🔴 Underrepresents seniors' contributions to society
  • 🟡 Only 13.9% of coverage celebrates their capabilities
  • 🟡 67.7% is neutral (untapped reframing opportunity)

The Central Question: How can advocates measure, track, and change media language about aging in real-time?

4. WHAT WE LEARNED

✨ Learning 1: Proof That Advocacy Works

2025 Breakthrough Empowering coverage jumped +4%
Limiting Language Dropped From 17.9% to 15.7% (-2.2%)
Timeline 2023 baseline → 2025 peak
Implication Media narratives ARE changeable

🗺️ Learning 2: Geographic Variation

  • Ontario: 6,506 articles (54.6% of coverage) with 19.5% limiting = largest opportunity for impact
  • British Columbia: 3,192 articles with 16.1% limiting = best practice model (can we replicate?)
  • Alberta: 2,124 articles with 18.6% limiting = middle ground, growth potential
  • Insight: Regional advocacy strategies must differ; BC approach should be studied nationally

📚 Learning 3: Topic-Specific Narratives

  • Social_Community stories: 27.7% empowering (seniors as community contributors)
  • Healthcare coverage: Only 10% empowering (crisis narratives dominate)
  • 8,080 neutral articles: Massive conversion opportunity (reframe factual stories with empowering angles)
  • Insight: Topic selection determines narrative tone—different stories need different strategies

🎯 Learning 4: Outlet Accountability

  • Top 5 worst outlets (Global, CityNews, CBC) account for 600+ limiting articles
  • Model outlets (The Spectator, Niagara Falls Review) consistently 19%+ empowering
  • Local vs. national: Small outlets demonstrate better narrative quality than major chains
  • Insight: Outlet accountability is trackable; we can measure progress per outlet

🚀 Learning 5: System Enables Strategy

  • One-time analysis ≠ strategy: Static reports become outdated; built production system instead
  • Weekly monitoring catches shifts: 2026 regression (25.8% limiting) would have been invisible without automation
  • Data-driven advocacy: Stop guessing, start measuring media response to campaigns
  • Insight: Continuous intelligence enables rapid response and strategic pivots
