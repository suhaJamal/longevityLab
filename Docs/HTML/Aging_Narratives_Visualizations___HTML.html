The Worsening Crisis: Distortion Rate Over Time

This chart shows how media distortion has accelerated over 3 years, with a dramatic 6.8% increase in the last 6 months.

Key Insight: The upward trend is clear and alarming. After relative stability in early 2023, distortion rates have climbed steadily, with the steepest increase occurring between January and July 2025.

The Language That Harms: Top 10 Most Distorted Phrases

These phrases are consistently misused, with 'Silver Tsunami' distorted in 98% of articles.

Key Insight: Notice that even empowering phrases like 'Productive Aging' and 'Aging Well' are being twisted into limiting narratives. The highest-volume phrases ('Aging Well' with 2,216 articles) have massive impact.
