💡 Key Research Insights

  • "Aging well" is the most distorted phrase — only 20.2% accuracy despite being labeled "empowering". Media uses positive language while actual content is often neutral or negative.
  • "Silver tsunami" has 98% mismatch rate — labeled as "limiting" but articles average +0.133 sentiment, suggesting the phrase is used sensationally even in balanced reporting.
  • Neutral phrases become limiting — 3,211 articles (28.4% of mismatches) use neutral language but have strongly negative sentiment (-0.631 avg), revealing hidden bias.
  • Empowering phrases underdeliver — 2,828 articles use empowering language but have neutral sentiment (+0.040), suggesting performative positivity without substance.
  • Regional variation exists — BC media is most accurate (58.8%), while Alberta shows highest distortion (46.6% accuracy), suggesting different editorial standards.
  • Overall negative bias — Average sentiment of -0.045 across all articles suggests Canadian media leans toward negative framing of aging, regardless of phrase choice.
